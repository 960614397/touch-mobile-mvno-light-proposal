The Opportunity

The people who pay most per gigabyte are the least protected.

About 26.5 million South Africans receive a social grant, 8.2 million of them the SRD grant, and around 3.6 million work in the informal sector. Most buy mobile prepaid, in small amounts, at the highest price per gigabyte. Scammers chase grant payouts, e-wallets and SIM swaps in the same communities. Nobody offers them a plan priced the way they budget, with protection included, through an agent who lives down the road. Touch Mobile can.

The flywheel: Touch's agents win the first-time customer at volume. A plan priced by the day, with fraud protection built in, gives them a reason to stay. Touch Back returns data and airtime on every rand, so the customer has every reason to step up.

01 · How It Works

An MVNO Light: Touch owns the brand, MVNE runs the plumbing

Touch Mobile is a light MVNO on MVNE's managed platform and MTN's network. It is API-first, needs minimal capex and has no network to build. Touch owns the customer, the brand, the prices and the agent network. MVNE runs provisioning, billing and RICA. Dash Host's hosting and domain stack runs the Touch storefront, and DSG's Digital Resilience and eInsurer supply the protection.
